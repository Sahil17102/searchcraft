Common questions

Everything you need
to move forward.

What is a courier aggregator platform?+

It brings multiple courier services into one system so a business can compare options, book shipments, print labels, track movement and manage billing without switching between portals.

Can SearchCraft Digital support multiple carrier partners?+

Yes. The interface is designed for multi-carrier workflows, including serviceability, rate comparison, assignment and unified tracking.

Is it suitable for franchises and client accounts?+

Yes. Separate client workspaces, access roles, configurable rates and account-level reporting are core parts of the platform concept.

Do I need to install any software?+

No desktop installation is required. SearchCraft Digital is designed as a secure browser-based platform.

Can my team request a tailored demo?+

Absolutely. Share your shipment volume and current workflow through the contact page, and the demo can focus on your operational needs.
